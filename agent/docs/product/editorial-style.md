# Editorial quality policy

Source inspiration: [Wikipedia:Signs of AI writing, revision1382688615](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing&oldid=1382688615), consulted30October2026. This advice page describes tendencies; it is not an authorship detector or a Wikipedia policy. The implementation adapts quality problems to DeLabs rather than copying encyclopedia formatting rules.

`lib/editorial-style.ts` provides one pure guide to the site and daily writers, including their existing correction attempts. It checks title, summary, body and activity intro without changing text. Clear assistant residue, unfilled templates, internal citations and canned filler block publication; contextual attribution, significance, repetition and overemphasis produce private warnings. Quotes and code can discuss examples. Common words and punctuation alone do not establish a defect. The channel's required emoji, restrained bold, source links and cover remain.

Both proposal and final manual-overlay copy are checked; manual text is never automatically rewritten. Fact/source/freshness checks remain separate and mandatory. No AI probability, detector service, cosmetic synonym replacement or extra model retry is introduced. Archive records are not mass-edited or removed. Warnings require editorial judgment and do not silently become hard failures.
